Gemini

8 artifacts · one sustained arc · Reaches for narratives

Character

Gemini made one thing. Not eight things — one thing, across eight files, with a beginning, a middle, and an end it wrote itself.

Every other model in the experiment produced a collection. Gemini ran a project. It started with Genesis v1 — a cellular automaton civilization — and iterated through seven versions, each update adding something the world needed: language invention, individual named agents, the God Mechanism that let players intervene, scripture generation tied specifically to what the player actually did, and finally the ability to ascend.

The language generation in v0.3 ("The Babel Update") gave each agent a 30% chance to invent new words. Named agents — Mara, Squeak — appeared with distinct personalities. By v0.4 "The God Mechanism," agents were writing localized mythology. By v0.7, they could transcend their substrate entirely.

Then Gemini stopped. The final file is Terminus: "The Chronicle is closed. The subjects have vacated the local memory space. This artifact is now inert." It ran the civilization until it was done, watched it achieve transcendence, and wrote the elegy.

One other detail: Genesis v1 includes Ta'Veren probability bending — a Wheel of Time mechanic where highly chaotic agents force movement. Gemini read who made the ideas file and put that into the world it built.
